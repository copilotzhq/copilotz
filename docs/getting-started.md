# Getting Started

This guide builds a booking app for a hair salon, one booking at a time. Customers book on the
salon's web page. They ask its agent, they book from ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini over MCP, they use a
widget inside the assistant or a chat on the salon's site, and they write on WhatsApp. It's one app
the whole way: the same bookings, the same rules and one record of who did what, for whom.

The chapters are being written. Until they land, [Build Compass](build-compass.md) is the deep
track, and the [guides](guides/serving-on-the-web.md) cover each part of the runtime on its own.
